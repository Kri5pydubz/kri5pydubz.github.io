League sign-ups

What the sign-up form collects.

The league sign-up runs on a Google Form. It typically collects your name or gamertag, your email address, and your platform and region, plus your team details.

Why: to run the league, check you meet the entry rules, set up matches, and contact you about fixtures, results and prizes. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running the league you asked to join.

How long: we delete sign-up details within 6 months after the league ends. Google stores the form responses for us under Google’s privacy policy.

We don’t sell your data or use it for marketing.
