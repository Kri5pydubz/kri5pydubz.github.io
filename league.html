How it works

New here? Easy. Every match is first to 2 rounds (best of 3) unless we say otherwise.

2 points for every game you win, plus 1 point for every round you win — so even a losing game can earn points. Rounds won minus rounds lost is your round difference. Level on points? Round difference decides it first, then most rounds won.
