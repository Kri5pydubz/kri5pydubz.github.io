League rules

January league. £50 on the line.

Plain-English summary, not legal advice.

Last updated: 13 October 2026. Questions? Email krispydubz@gmail.com.

Who can enter

Eligibility.

  • You must be 18 or over, or 13 to 17 with a parent or guardian’s consent.
  • Open to super fans and subscribers, based in the UK or the EU.
  • It’s free. No purchase needed to enter.
  • This is a game of skill, not chance.

When

Sundays, 9pm UK time.

Matches run on Sundays at 9pm UK time during the January league. Fixtures are posted on the League page and TikTok.

Wired (ethernet) connection only, no Wi-Fi. Both players on a team must play every league night; if one doesn’t show, the team doesn’t score that session.

How it’s won

Points decide it.

Every match is first to 2 rounds (best of 3) unless we say otherwise. You get 2 points for every game you win, plus 1 point for every round you win.

The team top of the table when the league ends wins. Level on points? Round difference decides it, then most rounds won.

Prize

£50 to the winners.

The winning team gets £50 cash, paid within 14 days of the league ending by a method agreed by email. Winners may need to confirm their age and identity privately before we pay. Champions also get a DUBZ champion badge and a shout-out on the League page.

Fair play

Cheat and you’re out.

Cheating, smurfing (playing on someone else’s or a second account to hide your level), or abuse towards anyone means disqualification. The organiser can change fixtures, settle disputes and update these rules if needed. The organiser’s decision is final.
